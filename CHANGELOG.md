# Changelog

## 2.0.0 – 2026-11-17

A complete rebuild of TeamCreatorr.

- **New design.** A fresh look with a light and dark theme, a floating menu with a big + button, and English, Dutch and Turkish.
- **Your phone's language.** The app uses your phone's language when it is English, Dutch or Turkish, and English otherwise. You can pick another language in Settings, or on Android 13 and newer in the Android settings for the app.
- **Fair teams, with duos and drag to swap.** Pick who plays and the app splits them into two even teams based on rating. Keep two players together or against each other, and drag a player onto someone in the other team to swap them.
- **Results with goal scorers.** Enter the score with − and +, and tap players to give them goals. Goals you don't assign count as "Unknown".
- **Standings with an FC-style rating.** Every player gets a card with a rating from 55 to 95. The table shows played, won, drawn, lost, goals, points and recent form.
- **Share as an image.** Send the line-up, the result and the standings to your group as a picture. Nothing is ever sent automatically.
- **Backups.** Save everything to one file and restore it later or on a new phone. Backups from 1.x can still be restored.
- **Privacy.** No account, no internet permission, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone, and profile photos never leave it.

### Upgrading from 1.x

Version 2.0 uses a new signing key, so it can't be installed over 1.x:

1. In the old app, make a backup (export).
2. Uninstall the old app.
3. Install TeamCreatorr 2.0.0.
4. Restore your backup (Settings → Restore backup).
