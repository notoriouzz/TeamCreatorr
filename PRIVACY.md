# Privacy statement – TeamCreatorr

_Last updated: November 2026_

TeamCreatorr is made by Notoriouzz. This statement explains what happens to your data. In short: **nothing leaves your phone unless you share it yourself.**

## What the app collects

Nothing. TeamCreatorr has no account, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The developer never receives any of your data.

## Internet

The app has no permission to use the internet, so it cannot send or receive data. There is one exception: the "Buy me a coffee" link in Settings opens the Ko-fi website in your own browser, outside the app.

## Where your data is stored

Players, matches, results, goals, points and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read this storage. Android's cloud backup is switched off for this app, so your data is not copied to Google or anywhere else.

## Profile photos

Photos you pick for players are scaled down and stored inside the app on your phone. They are **never** included in a backup file, a shared message or a shared image.

## Backups

You can make a backup file yourself (Settings → Make backup). You decide where to save it and who you give it to. The file contains your players, matches and settings, but no photos. It is not encrypted, so anyone who has the file can read it. Keep it somewhere safe.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share**, the app prepares a message or an image (line-up, result or standings) and opens Android's share menu. You choose the app and the recipients. Nothing is sent automatically.

## Deleting your data

You can delete players, matches or everything at any time in the app (Settings → Start over). Uninstalling the app removes all its data from your phone.

## Children

TeamCreatorr does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this statement changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

## Contact

Questions? Open an issue in the public TeamCreatorr repository on GitHub.
